# Workstreams (experimental)

::: warning Experimental
Turn on **Settings › Experimental: Workstreams** to try this. It is off by default.
:::

A **Workstream** is a named collection of sessions that you create — for example, every session working on one feature, across Projects and agents.

- A session belongs to at most one Workstream.
- **Archiving** a Workstream archives its sessions.
- **Deleting** a Workstream orphans its sessions: they go back to the Sessions Page, untouched.

## Two pages

With Workstreams on, HOA has two pages, switched from the page switcher:

| Page                 | Shows                                                                       |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sessions Page**    | Session History grouped by Project — only sessions that no Workstream owns. |
| **Workstreams Page** | Your Workstreams with their sessions, beside the selected session's chat.   |

Turning the setting off hides the page but keeps every Workstream and its sessions.
